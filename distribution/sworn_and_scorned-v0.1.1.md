# Sworn & Scorned 0.1.1 — PRE-ALPHA

The llama has acquired a grudge book. This first public candidate tracks evidence-backed grievances and promises from NPC perspectives, adds short reminders to later eligible conversations, and applies small affinity effects once per qualifying lifecycle transition.

## Included

- Opt-in cast of up to eight NPCs; asynchronous evaluation through OpenRouter Decisions (`typesafe/jev-1.13` by default) or a supported CHIM relationship LLM connector.
- Guarded ledgers, ordered jobs, retry/fallback handling, history snapshots, identity/scope revalidation and replay protection.
- Additive prompt notes capped at 240 characters, audience checks, pacing, mute and out-of-character dismissal.
- Journal, Director view, worker status/restart and ten source-backed directional lore seeds.
- MO2 sync ZIP, schema-4 DWPkg, repository-installer tar and SHA-256 checksums. No ESP/ESL or game companion.

## Verified scope

The final candidate passed 31 PHP syntax checks, 28 unit/regression scripts, five isolated database suites, two package checks, live-server simulated-client/page checks and all eight MP 0.1.17 / PCV 0.1.14 / SHARMAT 3.1.9.3 combinations. Fourteen synthetic cases ran through real Jev/Gemma adapters; their apology interpretation differed. See [the verification record](../docs/verification/release-0.1.1.md).

## Limits

PRE-ALPHA. Gameplay, dialogue quality, long sessions, save migration, production concurrency/latency and automatic MO2 transfer are unverified. Intervening counterpart exchanges split evaluation context. Saved keys are unencrypted in CHIM's database; use a dedicated spending-limited credential. The plugin ships disabled and without a key. Removal does not undo stored ledgers or affinity.

Choose one installation route, verify the installed version in CHIM's Plugin Manager, then enable and configure. [Full guide and downloads](../README.md)
