# Richer records — 0.1.9 PRE-ALPHA

This candidate preserves the tone correction and records the concrete wrong or accepted obligation, its parties, and any stated settlement. Publication does not install it on the gaming server. Existing records are readable without invented historical terms, migration writes or automatic affinity changes.

## Evidence contract

- Exact witnessed utterances, speakers and event references are facts about what was said, not proof that an inventory transfer or accusation is true.
- Nature is an interpreted category. Completing an accepted obligation is a supported settlement expectation; an explicit alternative/remedy requires its own exact source. Unstated quantities, items, deadlines and remedies stay unknown.
- An immutable subject anchors each record. The full bounded originating exchange preserves terms spread across lines; it may include unrelated topics, which are not automatically terms of this issue. Subsequent evidence cannot rewrite this substance.
- A favour alone creates no obligation. A concrete accepted commitment is required.
- A valid accepted replacement has a distinct issue and reciprocal link. Its predecessor becomes released with zero affinity, retaining the original substance. Missing, invalid or skipped replacements do not release the predecessor.
- Typed source-label choices work through the existing Jev and CHIM relationship-LLM adapters. No free-text extraction service, dependency or extra model round trip was added. Confidence thresholds and affinity rules remain unchanged.

## Limits

New records require the complete witnessed origin exchange to fit eight utterances, each at most 640 characters. Larger or incomplete exchanges abstain from new creation rather than silently drop possible terms; existing issue updates still run. This is an evidence budget, not an NPC cast cap. Cross-batch acceptance remains an existing limitation.

The full journal/evaluator retain bounded context. The conversation reminder remains at most240 characters and may elide supporting terms; the short split-term fixture retains both amount and deadline. This is not an accounting subsystem: partial payments retain the original obligation and do not settle its whole balance automatically.

Tests cover up to two simultaneous obligations in model classification. Accuracy with24 simultaneously addressed issues and the1, 024-token response budget is not established; truncated responses abstain and may miss updates. Provider failures remain subject to existing retry/fallback handling. Synthetic dialogue and acknowledgement rows do not certify actual Skyrim inventory actions, audible delivery, long gameplay or universal interpretation accuracy. Historical live grievances are untouched.
